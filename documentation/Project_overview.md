# Project Overview: Maternal & Child Health (MCH) County Analytics

## Background

Maternal and Child Health (MCH) remains a major public health priority in Kenya. County-level healthcare analysis is essential for identifying regional disparities, monitoring service delivery, and supporting evidence-based interventions.

This project demonstrates the use of healthcare analytics and data visualization techniques to transform structured clinical datasets into actionable public health insights.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze Maternal & Child Health indicators at county level
- Identify geographic disparities and mortality trends
- Visualize healthcare risk patterns using interactive dashboards
- Demonstrate end-to-end healthcare analytics workflow
- Support data-driven public health decision-making

---

## Key Questions Answered

- Which counties have the highest under-5 mortality rates?
- How does maternal healthcare service coverage vary across counties?
- Which regions show elevated healthcare risk levels?
- Where are postnatal care and facility delivery gaps most significant?

---

## Intended Users

- Public Health Officers
- County Health Management Teams
- Healthcare Analysts
- NGOs and Development Programs
- Recruiters and Portfolio Reviewers

---

## Data Description

Synthetic county-level Maternal & Child Health datasets were generated to simulate realistic Kenyan healthcare reporting patterns while preserving data privacy and confidentiality.

Indicators included:
- Under-5 Mortality
- Facility Delivery Coverage
- Antenatal Care (ANC)
- Postnatal Care (PNC)
- Skilled Birth Attendance
